I contributed to every aspect of the design, build, integration, and testing of our robot “Cubie.” My work included organizing the shared Google Drive, developing the bill of materials, budget, and overall strategy, and helping guide the system-level approach we used throughout the project. I helped assemble the robot, wired the breadboard, and wrote and integrated code as part of the full system build. I also designed the roboarms and license plate in Fusion and coordinated the laser cutting for those parts through RPL. I also helped assemble, test, and integrate sensors and other components. I created the full CAD assembly for our robot design. For the final report, I wrote the Design Process Reflection, contributed to appendices (BOM and CAD assembly), and supported overall project integration and documentation.
